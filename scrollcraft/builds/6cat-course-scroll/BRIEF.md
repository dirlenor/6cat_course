2. **Journey:** “ค่อยๆเล่าเรื่องด้วยการ scroll”. The existing course story remains in order: Zero to Live, the problem, the learning approach, the workflow, proof, instructor, and booking.
3. **Energy curve:** “ช่วงที่ พีคควรเป้นช่วง section 2-4”.
4. **Feeling and memorable moment:** “อยากให้ คนที่เจ้ามาใช้รู้สึกตื่นเต้น” and “อยากให้คนจำว่าการ หแroll ทำให้เว็ปดมีชีวิตมากกว่าเดิม”.
5. **Signature-move seed:** “อยากให้ มีการ scale iูปภาพ ตอน scroll ได้”.
6. **Aesthetic range:** “playful เล่นเว็ปนี้แล้วรูสึกสนึก”.
7. **World structure:** “ให้ทั้งหน้าเป้นหลายๆฉากตัดอารมไปเรื่อยๆ แต่ยังเล่าเรื่องเดียวกันอยู่”.
8. **Assets:** “สามารถใช้ asset ได้เลย”.

## Chosen grammar

**Chaptered scenes with a playful visual system.** The page cuts between distinct scenes while preserving one course story and one orange, black, and warm-paper world. This is not a continuous-world build because the brief explicitly asks for emotional scene cuts.

## Proposed journey

1. **Invitation:** Zero to Live makes the promise feel immediate.
2. **Friction:** The visitor recognises why generic AI tutorials fail on their own project.
3. **Turn:** The course shifts the feeling from stuck to capable.
4. **Motion peak:** A horizontal workflow rail becomes the proof that they can move from reference to live.
5. **Proof:** Reviews settle the excitement into confidence.
6. **Trust:** The instructor gives the story a human guide.
7. **Resolve:** Pricing turns readiness into one clear booking action.

## Feeling curve

1. **Curiosity:** The initial promise assembles around the Zero to Live image.
2. **Recognition:** The problem scene names the visitor’s own frustration with generic tutorials.
3. **Delight:** The course approach transforms that stuck feeling into a playful visual change.
4. **Awe (peak):** The workflow rail travels sideways while the course imagery scales under scroll.
5. **Confidence:** Real learner reviews give the high-energy movement evidence.
6. **Trust:** The instructor scene slows down and becomes personal.
7. **Resolve:** The booking scene holds a single direct action.

## Tell-someone sentence

“It’s the course site where scrolling turns a stuck idea into a live website.”

## Signature move

**The Live Trace:** A persistent orange route line collects a node for each chapter as the visitor scrolls. It accelerates and opens into the horizontal workflow rail at the peak, then resolves into the booking action.

The registry was empty when this brief was created, so no prior build constrains this plan.
